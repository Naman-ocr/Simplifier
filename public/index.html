Unit Mapping

Units are auto-normalized by default (NOS→Nos, KGS→Kgs, MTR→Mtr). Enable unit conversion only if your client records in a different unit than the invoice — e.g. invoice shows MTR but Tally records in KGS with a conversion factor.
